# Privacy

GEDCOM Privacy Guard is free software from
[Vashon Software](https://vashonsoftware.com). This page says what
happens to your information when you use the website,
[gedcomtools.com](https://gedcomtools.com), or the program you install.
The short version: your family tree file stays on your computer, the
site sets no cookies, and we count visits without knowing who you are.

## Your family tree file

- **It's never uploaded.** When you choose a file on the website, your
  browser reads it and the checking runs inside the page itself. The
  file, its notes and the results never go to us or to anyone else.
- **Your browser enforces that.** The site tells your browser (with its
  *Content Security Policy*) to refuse any connection except to the
  site's own files and the visit counter described below, so even a
  mistake in our code couldn't send your file anywhere.
- **What you download is made on your computer.** The review
  spreadsheet and the cleaned file are created in your browser and saved
  where your browser saves downloads.
- **The installed program has no network code at all.** It reads the
  file you name and writes its results next to it.

[Why your data is safe](safety.md) explains how we check all of this and
how you can check it yourself.

## What the site keeps on your computer

- **No cookies,** and no advertising or tracking tools.
- **The program files, for offline use.** The site saves its own files
  (the page, the checking engine and the standard rules) in your
  browser's storage, so it starts faster and works without a
  connection. Nothing from your tree is stored. Clearing this site's
  data in your browser removes them.

## What we learn about visits

- **Visit counts.** We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which records that
  a page was viewed: its address, the site that linked to it, your
  browser type, your country and how fast the page loaded. It sets no
  cookies, doesn't identify you and doesn't follow you to other sites.
  We see only totals.
- **Hosting.** The site is hosted by [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
  Like any web host, it handles your computer's internet address to
  deliver the pages and to block attacks. We don't keep server logs of
  our own, and we never sell or share information about visitors.

## When you contact us

- **Problem reports and feedback** go through GitHub issues, which are
  public, so GitHub's [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
  applies to them. The "copy a problem report" button only puts text on
  your clipboard: version numbers, your browser type and counts about
  your file, never names, notes, places or dates. You choose whether to
  paste it anywhere.
- **Please never send us your real family file.** If you want to show
  us a problem, use a small made-up example instead.
- **Security problems** can be reported privately; see
  [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).
